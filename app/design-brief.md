# Univers in Pink
Design read: A joyful, cinematic all-pink boutique for women who want everyday objects with personality.
Concept spine: A planet where everything is pink, entered through a tactile celestial portal.
Delivery tier: cinema, cinematic entrance with interactive planet and camera journey.
Animation mode: animated-website
Journey shape: single-shot
Journey: orbit a pearl-pink globe, approach its rose-cloud atmosphere, descend toward an architectural pink boutique.
World grammar: satin rose clouds, pearl reflections, spherical worlds, blush stone, no purple or gold.
Mobile framing: centered planet and boutique, expendable edges, portrait-safe focal points.
Narrative: clicking the globe begins entry, the visitor can scrub the flight or skip directly to shopping.
Desktop byte budget: 20 MiB. Mobile byte budget: 12 MiB.
Palette: #fff0f5 blush ground, #f7c9dc surfaces, #ce285f cherry accent, #57172f ink, #8d4a64 muted.
Palette defense: pink is the user's explicit product and identity constraint, tonal pink carries every section.
Type: Outfit display and system sans body, geometric rounded personality without sugary scripts.
Corners: 16px product media, circular planet controls, straight editorial separators.
Theme paradigm: Bold Studio Solid, pink monochrome.
Background character: full-bleed cinematic imagery.
Typography character: clean geometric grotesk.
Hero architecture: massive image-first globe, restrained left-offset headline.
Section system: gallery-led cadence.
Signature components: layered image crop frames, diagonal staggered masonry, hover-accordion slices, brand marquee.
Second-read moment: macro crop carrying the brand color on the home collection.
Previous build axes: no previous build in this conversation.
Section plan:
1. Planet entrance: full viewport image canvas, headline and circular entry hit area.
2. Camera flight: semantic scrub journey with skip shopping control.
3. Collection portals: asymmetric two-by-two photographic gallery with mobile two columns.
4. Product edit: responsive four/two-column catalog, category and shade filtering.
5. Pink living: offset image/statement split, stacked on mobile.
6. Footer: oversized wordmark, practical information dialog links.
CTA inventory:
Enter world: globe hit area and orbit ring, pointer tilt, click imprints and advances into journey.
Skip intro: slim text control with translated arrow.
Collections: full image tiles lift on hover and select catalog category.
View product: entire product media opens accessible details dialog.
Favorite: heart toggle with pressed state.
Add to bag: wide slab with tactile press, quantity feedback.
Bag: outlined bag glyph with count, drawer and remove/quantity controls.
Replay: circular arrow affordance returning to entrance.
Asset plan: 6-panel storyboard; collection and product design boards; single continuous film; pink product images; logo; icon sheet; section plate; branded launch cover and favicon family.
Commerce: clearly labeled sample catalog; saved bag and favorites; no fake checkout or invented fulfillment promises.
Accessibility: keyboard controls, focus trapping, Escape dialogs, reduced-motion static story and no automatic audio.

Entrance correction: Clicking Enter opens a modal and plays the complete film via the user gesture. It never scrolls the page on a timer. The film ended event reveals an explicit Enter boutique action over the final frame. A separate Skip film action can bypass it. Reduced-motion preference does not silently redirect an explicit request to play the film. Media failures expose retry and skip controls.
